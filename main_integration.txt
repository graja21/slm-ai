1) Copy the folder financial_engine and the file financial_router.py into your ai-backend root.

2) In your existing main.py, add:

from financial_router import router as financial_router

app.include_router(financial_router)

3) Remove or rename your old @app.post("/financial-pdf-chunked") endpoint to avoid a duplicate route.

4) Restart:
python -m uvicorn main:app --reload
